Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Our Zero-Data Collection Commitment

LDLx Security Kiosk OS is engineered from the ground up for absolute data sovereignty. I do not track, store, or process any personal telemetry, diagnostic logs, or user usage data on my servers. Your local database operations remain strictly offline and isolated within your controlled environment.

2. Payment Processing via Stripe

The only circumstance under which data interaction occurs is when you purchase Pro subscriptions or commercial licenses for my services. All financial transactions, billing records, and payment credentials are processed securely through my designated purchasing partner, Stripe, Inc.

I never store credit card numbers, banking details, or full billing profiles on LDLx systems. Please review Stripe's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle your payment data.

3. Open Source Transparency

Because core architectural components of LDLx are released under open-source parameters, you have complete visibility into how the operating system executes. No hidden background trackers or telemetry agents exist within my source distributions.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling during Stripe subscription checkout, please reach out via my GitHub repository.
